LIVING AREAS & BEDROOMS

Dusting & Surfaces

Dust ceiling corners and remove cobwebs
Dust ceiling fan blades and light fixtures
Wipe down all light switches and outlet covers
Dust and wipe all shelves, moving items to clean underneath
Dust baseboards, moldings, door frames and window sills
Polish mirrors, glass surfaces and chrome fixtures
Dust TV screens and electronics (gently) - no wet wiping
Wipe wall edges and corners for dust buildup
Spot clean walls and scuff marks
Dust ceiling fans throughout home
Clean air vents and return covers
Windows & Blinds

Wipe down window sills and tracks
Clean interior window glass
Dust or wipe blinds (each slat)
Wipe curtain rods and rings
Floors & Furniture

Vacuum/sweep underneath the furniture
Vacuum under and between sofa cushions
Vacuum all carpets, rugs and edges. Also underneath if possible
Sweep and mop all hard floors
Wipe down doors, door handles and frames
Wipe baseboards
Bedrooms

Change and launder bed linens if asked
Dust and wipe nightstands, dressers and headboard
Organize visible surfaces and put back as they were
KITCHEN

Counters & Surfaces

Clear and wipe all countertops
Clean backsplash and grout lines
Wipe cabinet fronts, handles and under-cabinet areas
Appliances

Clean inside and outside of microwave
Wipe down oven exterior and control panel
Clean fridge exterior and door seals
Clean dishwasher exterior and door edge
Degrease range hood, filters and vents
Move small appliances and clean underneath
Wipe down toaster, kettle, coffee maker exteriors
Sink & Fixtures

Deep clean sink, faucet and drain
Remove and clean sink strainer
Wash dish soap bottle and any counter accessories
BATHROOMS

Toilet

Scrub inside of toilet bowl
Clean toilet seat, lid, base and tank exterior
Unscrew and remove toilet seat, wash both pieces separately, reattach
Clean behind and around base of toilet
Shower & Tub

Scrub shower/tub walls, floor and fixtures
Remove soap scum and hard water buildup
Clean shower tiles and grout
Clean shower door, tracks and seals
Wipe shower rod if applicable
Clean and polish showerhead and taps
Sink & Vanity

Clean sink, faucet and drain
Wipe vanity countertop and backsplash
Clean mirror (streak-free)
Wipe cabinet fronts
Organize visible surfaces and put back as they were
Walls, Fixtures & Floors

Clean exhaust fan cover and vent
Wipe light fixtures and switches
Fold toilet paper
Put ruugs back when the floor is dry
HALLWAYS & STAIRS

Stairs

Dust railings, spindles and ledges
Vacuum each stair tread and riser
Vacuum stair edges and corners
Mop hard-surface stairs
Wipe handrail thoroughly
EXTRA TOUCHES

Walls & Doors

Wipe down doors and windows at nose/touch height (especially with pets)
General

Wipe down all garbage bin exteriors
Organize and tidy visible areas throughout home - blankets, throws and pillows
Refresh the overall look and feel of the home
Do a full walk-through final check of every room
